Eligibility rules

MEPs, APAs, and Staff have different budgetary approval chains. Double-check that your role matches your official profile to prevent automated rejection.

Transit thresholds

Direct trains or shuttle buses between Brussels, Strasbourg, and Luxembourg are fully reimbursed. Private car usage requires prior written authorization from your supervisor.

Nightly ceilings

Maximum nightly hotel allowances: Brussels (€180), Strasbourg (€210), Luxembourg (€190). Agency-booked hotels automatically include standard breakfast packages.

Special arrangements

The European Parliament covers requests for adapted transit and accessible accommodation for personnel with documented disabilities.
